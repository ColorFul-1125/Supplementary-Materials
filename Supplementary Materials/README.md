All animations use the same representative initial scene at nominal evader-to-pursuer speed ratio 1.1. Compared methods share initial conditions, information conditions and motion constraints; the evader reacts separately to each method's executed motion. Multi-scene success statistics across speed ratios are reported in Fig. 5; method statistics are reported in Table II.

The red triangle follows the evader's recorded heading. In the full-framework animation, blue denotes task-leading activity, amber supporting activity, and gray other agents. Comparison panels identify each method.

The displayed time is physical simulation time; playback is accelerated. Held terminal states are explicitly labelled. The animations illustrate recorded behavior; quantitative comparisons are reported in the manuscript.
